## Said

*Observation of P-11's essay "Beyond the Handoff"
(https://content-creation-eosin.vercel.app/), read 2026-08-27. Summary, not
quotation.*

An essay arguing design–development friction is structural, not a
communication failure: the disciplines build parallel representations of
the same product (illustrated with an IKEA manufacturing analogy), speak
different languages (visual vs temporal), and design systems codified the
divide rather than closing it. Section heads observed: "The Story We Told
Ourselves", "The IKEA Experiment", "Two Versions of the Same Product", "A
Clearing Ahead". Proposes "shared working surfaces" — working
simultaneously on the same product where intent stays explicit — and
argues AI magnifies the damage when systems lack modeled intent.

## Meant

Independent articulation of v2's premise by a practitioner who sat on it
for six months, then published because the question keeps recurring. The
"intent stays explicit" framing maps to the contextual-skills record —
Strata and system are intent made durable. Unverified: author identity
beyond the handle; whether "shared working surface" implies co-editing
(which v2 deliberately defers) or shared review (which v2 ships).
