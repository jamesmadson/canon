## Meant

The handoff question James raised, answered from inside a large product
org: handoff stops being a moment and becomes co-ownership of one
artifact, and the enabling condition is social — a named engineering
buddy who reviews and explains failures — not tooling. Second independent
voice for design conversation living in PRs (after the thread's P-04).
Reads as validation that v2's graduation path is "open a PR from the
prototype," and that the product should make the buddy relationship
legible rather than pretend to replace it. Unverified: how many of the
twenty kept building after the workshop, and full text unread — summary
fetch only.
