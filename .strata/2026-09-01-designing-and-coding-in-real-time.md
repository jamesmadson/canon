corporate in-house seat rather than the thread's mix — but "share them
with the entire team" is asserted without saying *how* the team receives
them, which is exactly the gap the thread's four voices named. The
sharing mechanism going unspecified in a celebration post is consistent
with the review-surface loss, not evidence against it.

designing-in-code signal was web; this is SwiftUI in the simulator.
One voice, not a pattern — but it moves native prototyping from zero
voices to one, which bears on the prototype model's tier note (native
degrades to captures-plus-record; a simulator prototype cannot take the
annotation script).

Third: "rounds of iteration just to get back to the original intent" is
the drift argument for prototypes-as-source stated from the buyer's
