## Said

*Observation, 2026-08-28: Anthropic's "The AI-Native SDLC Playbook"
(claude.com/blog, Louis Claxton, 2026-08-21) — a published, bylined
reference, cited rather than pseudonymized. Read via summary fetch.*

Six stages — Plan, Design, Build, Test, Deploy, Maintain — reimagined
for agent-speed work: intent captured as version-controlled `intent.md`
files; organizational skills encoding policy; agents verifying their own
work before human review; hooks as deterministic approval gates;
monitoring findings re-entering the pipeline as new intent artifacts.

> "Code is no longer the bottleneck — the human-speed steps around it
> are."

> "Every stage commits an artifact the next stage can read."

## Meant

Two readings. (1) Canon-the-product is the design lane of this cycle,
stage by stage: Strata evidence and the decision log feed Plan;
prototypes are Build artifacts; review runs are Test; PR graduation is
Deploy; staleness flags are Maintain feeding back to Plan. The playbook
hands alpha conversations a legitimizing frame from Anthropic's own
literature. (2) Canon-the-practice already runs it — specs, plans,
worktree agents, review gates, the decision log as audit trail — which
means the claim "we work this way" is demonstrable, not aspirational.
Unverified: full text unread (summary fetch); worth a complete read
before citing it in outreach.
