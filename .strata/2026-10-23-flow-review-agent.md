## Said

*LinkedIn post, seen 2026-10-23 (screenshot; author not recorded, post date unknown).*

> Towards the end of work on a new feature, most of it is already live in
> Claude Code. That is, on my computer. The PM can't see it. The other
> designer can't see it, and neither can the developers. In Figma, this is
> never a problem, because the file is the work and everyone can come in and
> see … There was a URL that I updated with every progress and everyone had a
> link, but you can only see one screen at a time, and you can't comment. For
> those who are used to seeing all the different screens and states spread
> out and arranged according to the flow, it was not easy to digest.
>
> I was thinking of exporting the screens to Figma - but it's slow and you
> have to go through and fix all the deviations that are created. Adopting a
> new tool just for that felt overkill. In the end: I created a specific agent
> whose job is to thoroughly go through all the screens according to the
> different flows and collect screenshots, and an artifact that displays
> everything in order. I added a link to the working prototype, the option for
> team members to leave comments, and tracking versions. Claude allows me to
> direct comments to him and he immediately starts implementing them.
>
> … the biggest change we are making as designers or product people right now
> is not learning new tools and different work processes, but rather a
> conceptual change from looking for existing solutions to creating the
> solutions that are right for us.

## Meant

The same gap as P-09 and P-10: work built in code lives on one machine, and a
single live URL shows one screen at a time with no comments. What they built
by hand is close to Canon's board: every screen and state laid out by flow, a
link back to the running prototype, comments, versions, and comments routed
straight to the agent that builds. Two readings, both unverified: the agent
walking the flows is the expensive part, and they'd rather not adopt "a new
tool just for that", so Canon has to arrive from inside the agent session
rather than as another app. That second point supports the Canon MCP.
