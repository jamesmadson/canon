## Meant

The market reaching for its own analogy — and choosing the review-and-
approval category, not the design-tool category. Frame.io is comments
anchored to the artifact (a frame of video) plus share links for people
without the editing tool: structurally the annotation layer's shape.
"Frame.io for coded prototypes" is instantly legible positioning
shorthand, with two corrections it invites: Canon references live
branches rather than hosting uploaded versions, and feedback becomes a
durable evidence record rather than an approve/reject workflow.
Unverified: the commenter's intent — analogy as praise, as "this exists
already," or as a wish.
